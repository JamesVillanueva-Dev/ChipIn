Read the setup out loud, then give one task at a time. Don't help. Press Reset before each new person. The log records every tap, including taps on gray buttons.

On a phone, this panel stays hidden. Hold two fingers on the screen for a second to open it. Reset closes it so you can hand the phone over.

Setup

You're Marcus, the banker at a Friday game. You hold everyone's money and pay them at the end.

Tasks

  1. You forgot what a red chip is worth. Find out.
  2. Diego lost his stack and wants to buy back in for $10, but only in green $1 chips. Log his rebuy.
  3. The game's over. Go to cash out and figure out why the totals don't match.
  4. Fix Jordan's count so the totals match. (Jordan actually has 3 green chips.)
  5. Pay Diego what his chips are worth.
